Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. MyNextSet works without registration and without an internet connection.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone, iPad or Android phone. We never receive it and cannot recover it for you. Deleting the app also deletes its data.

How do I move my plans to a new phone?

iPhone and iPad: iOS device transfer and iCloud or computer backups include MyNextSet data.

Android: MyNextSet does not use Google Drive backup. Before switching phones, export the library from Settings → Import and export and each plan from its menu with Export JSON, then import the files on the new phone from the same screen.

Completed workouts can be exported as JSON to keep or analyse them, but they cannot be imported back.

How do I import a plan from a photo or PDF?

  1. Open Plans, tap Import with AI (or New plan → Import with AI) and then Copy prompt.
  2. Paste the prompt into the AI chat you prefer and attach the photo or PDF of your plan.
  3. Copy the answer, go back to MyNextSet, paste it and tap Check JSON.
  4. Check the preview and tap Import plans.

How do I build a Tabata circuit?

Open Timer → Tabata, set the values and tap Add another Tabata for each block. Set the rest between Tabatas and the repetitions, then start the sequence or save it with a name.

I can't hear the beeps

In MyNextSet open the settings (the gear icon), make sure Sounds is on and turn up the volume. Beeps play while the app is in the foreground: the screen stays on during the timer for this reason.

How do I change the language?

MyNextSet is available in English and Italian and uses your phone's language. To choose a language for the app only: on iPhone and iPad open Settings → Apps → MyNextSet → Language; on Android 13 or later Settings → Apps → MyNextSet → Language.

Where is the guide?

In MyNextSet tap the gear icon and open Tutorial and guide.

Which version do I have?

On iPhone: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → MyNextSet. On Android: Settings → Apps → MyNextSet. It is also shown on the app page in the App Store or Google Play.
