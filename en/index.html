Your workout, one set at a time

MyNextSet is the app for iPhone, iPad and Android to build your workout plans, follow every set with guided rest and train with the Tabata timer. No account and no ads: your data stays on your device.

What you can do

  • Today: your scheduled plan, the weekly calendar and your completed workouts.
  • Plans and programs: warm-up, workout and stretching; straight sets, supersets, jump sets, drop sets, clusters and compound sets; programs with dates and their plans.
  • Guided workout: log reps, loads and times for every set, with rest that starts on its own.
  • Import with AI: turn a photo or PDF of your plan into a MyNextSet plan, with a preview before saving.
  • Tabata timer: work, rest, rounds and sets, circuits with several Tabatas, a beep before each phase change and saved timers.
  • Stats: summaries by period, records and progress for each exercise.
  • Library: ready-made calisthenics and gym exercises, plus the parameters you want to log.
  • JSON files: export and share plans, the exercise library and completed workouts.
  • Themes and sounds: lime green, blue or neon pink, and several alarm tones.

Import a plan from a photo

  1. In MyNextSet open Plans → Import with AI and copy the prompt.
  2. Paste it into the AI chat you prefer, together with the photo or PDF of your plan.
  3. Paste the answer into MyNextSet, check the preview and save.

MyNextSet does not connect to any AI service: you choose the chat and what to share.

Privacy

MyNextSet does not collect any personal data, uses no ads or tracking and requires no sign-up. Everything you enter stays on your device.
