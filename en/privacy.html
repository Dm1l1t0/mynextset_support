Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026 · Versione italiana

Summary: MyNextSet does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts and uses no servers, advertising, analytics or tracking. Your plans, workouts and settings stay on your device.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the MyNextSet app for iPhone and iPad, distributed on the App Store, and for Android, distributed on Google Play. The controller is the developer of MyNextSet, who publishes the app under the name shown on its store page. Contact: jtengineeringsupport@gmail.com.

2. Data we do not collect

The app does not send anything to us or to third parties: it has no internet access. In particular, it does not collect:

  • name, email address, phone number or other contact information;
  • health, fitness or workout data;
  • location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data;
  • device or advertising identifiers, usage data, crash reports;
  • any data for advertising or for tracking across apps and websites.

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or sign-in libraries.

3. Data stored on your device

To work, the app stores what you enter on your device: plans, programs, exercises and parameters, completed workouts, calendar, saved timers and preferences such as theme and alarm tone. This data stays under your control and we cannot see it.

  • Deletion: you can delete plans, workouts and timers in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.
  • Backup on iPhone and iPad: if you use iCloud or computer backups, iOS may include the app's data in those backups. This service is managed by Apple according to your settings.
  • Backup on Android: the app does not use Google Drive backup.

4. Export and sharing

You can export or share plans, the exercise library and completed workouts as JSON files. This only happens when you choose to, through the system save and share sheets. The app, service or person you choose as the destination handles the file under its own terms. Likewise, the app only opens files you select.

5. Import with AI

The Import with AI feature copies an instruction text to the clipboard. You paste it, together with a photo or PDF of your plan, into an external chat service of your choice, and then paste the answer back into MyNextSet. The app does not connect to that service and sends it no data. What you share with the external service is governed by its own privacy policy. We recommend not including health or personal data you do not want to share.

6. Permissions and links

The app does not request access to location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data or notifications. Timer alerts use the device audio, and the screen stays on while a timer is running. From the settings you can open the support email in your mail app and these pages in your browser.

7. If you contact us

If you email support, we use your address and message only to reply, keep them only as long as needed to handle your request, never use them for marketing and never share them with third parties. Email is handled through an external email provider (Gmail). You can ask us to delete the correspondence at any time.

8. Children

The app does not collect data from any user, including children.

9. Your rights

Since the app collects no personal data, we hold no app data to access, correct or delete: you manage it directly on your device. For email data you can exercise your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) by writing to jtengineeringsupport@gmail.com, and you may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

10. Changes

If the app changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the date above before releasing the new version.
